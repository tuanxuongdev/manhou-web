FAQ

Questions people ask

More on the support page

Is Manhou free? Does it add a watermark?

It’s free. Photos and videos you save have no logo or watermark, and no ads appear while you edit.

Which app makes ID photos at the right print size?

Manhou has 11 ID sizes (Vietnamese 3×4, 4×6, 2×3, passport, CCCD, and US, Schengen, Japan, Korea and China visas). Save the PDF sheet and print at 100% (Actual size) so each photo comes out true to size.

Does background removal need internet?

Only once: the person-matting model (about 36 MB) is downloaded the first time. After that, cut-outs run on your phone and nothing is uploaded.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos and videos are read from your library and saved back to it; every tool runs on the phone.

Do I have to give access to my whole library?

No. Allow all photos, only some, or use your phone’s photo picker with no permission at all.

Is Manhou on iPhone?

Manhou is on Android, from Google Play. There is no iPhone version yet.

Does it work on low-end phones?

Yes. Manhou reads your phone’s memory on launch and sizes previews, AI processing and exports to match: with 4 GB of RAM it exports photos up to 8 MP and video at 720p, with 6 GB up to 24 MP and 1080p, with 8 GB or more up to 50 MP and 4K. Very large photos are scaled down on export, and the app tells you first.

White or blue background?

It depends on the office. Vietnamese citizen ID and passports usually take white; many other forms accept blue. Manhou does both, plus grey, red and any colour.

How big is a 3×4 photo?

3×4 is 3 cm × 4 cm (30 × 40 mm); 4×6 is 4 cm × 6 cm. Manhou’s PDF sheet keeps those sizes when printed at 100%.

Can I retouch an ID photo?

Yes, lightly. ID photo has Studio light (balances exposure and skin colour, tones down shine) and Light retouch (off by default); it never reshapes the face. If the office asks for an unedited photo, leave both off.

Background removal vs. background blur?

Removal replaces the background with a colour or transparency; blur keeps it but softens it. Manhou does both.

Can I save a transparent PNG?

Yes. Choose the clear background and save as PNG, for stickers or other designs.
