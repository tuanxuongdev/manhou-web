Frequently asked questions

Is Manhou free?

Yes. Every on-device tool is free, with no ads and no logo on the photos or videos you save.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos and videos are read from your gallery and saved back to it, and every tool runs on the phone. A few optional tools can use cloud AI, but they are off by default and only run if you turn them on.

Do I need an internet connection?

Most tools work offline. Some AI tools download a model the first time you use them; after that they work offline too.

Is Manhou on iPhone?

Manhou is on Android, from Google Play. There is no iPhone version yet.

How do I print ID photos at the right size?

Save the print sheet as a PDF and print at 100 % (Actual size) with “Fit to page” off. Each photo comes out exactly 3×4, 4×6 or the size you chose. Photo labs can print from the same file.

Does it work on low-end phones?

Yes. Manhou sizes its work to the phone’s RAM. On a 4 GB phone, very large photos are scaled down on export, and the app tells you first.
