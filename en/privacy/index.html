Privacy

Effective 28 October 2026

Manhou is a photo and video editor that works on your phone. This page says plainly what happens to your photos and data.

Everything happens on your phone

Editing, beauty, background removal, captions and every other tool run on this phone. Manhou has no account and no server that receives your photos or videos.

Your photos never leave your phone

Photos and videos you open are read from your gallery and saved back to it. They are never uploaded. Some tools download an AI model the first time you use them, from the model’s public host; that download only brings a file to your phone and sends nothing of yours.

Cloud AI is off unless you turn it on

A few optional tools can use cloud AI (Google Gemini). They are off by default. Only if you turn them on in Settings and confirm is the photo you are working on sent, for that one request.

Crash logs stay on this phone

When the app hits an error it saves a short log on this phone (Settings › Crash log), with photo and file names removed. It is never sent automatically: it leaves the phone only if you share it yourself.

Permissions

Photos: to open your pictures and save your edits. Camera: to take photos and videos in the app. Microphone: to record sound when you film a video. Each one is asked for only when you first need it, and you can change it any time in your phone’s settings.

No ads, no tracking

Manhou shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking. We don’t collect, sell or share personal data.

About this website

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics or tracking, and loads no fonts or scripts from third parties. Your light / dark theme choice is stored only in your browser.
